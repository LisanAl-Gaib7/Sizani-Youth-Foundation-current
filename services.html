Our Programmes

Three programmes that work together to support every learner.

Tutoring

After-School Tutoring

Small-group sessions in maths, English and science, run by qualified and volunteer teachers straight after school. Sessions run three afternoons a week, with extra Saturday classes before exams.

Mentorship

Mentorship

Every learner in our senior programme is paired with a trained volunteer mentor. Mentors meet with learners regularly to build confidence and help them plan for life after school.

Digital Skills

Digital Literacy

Short courses covering typing, using email safely, basic office software, and researching further study or jobs online. Learners get a certificate when they finish the course.
