About Us

Learn how Sizani Youth Foundation started and what we believe in.

Our Story

Sizani Youth Foundation was started in 2016 by a group of local teachers who began running weekend tutoring sessions for learners in their community. What began as informal, volunteer-led lessons has grown into a structured programme that now supports over 300 learners every year.

Today, that same spirit still drives everything we do: teachers, mentors and volunteers giving their time so that young people in under-resourced communities get the support they need.

Our Mission

To help young people in under-resourced communities reach their full potential through tutoring, mentorship, and digital skills.

Our Vision

A generation of confident, capable youth who are equipped for further study and employment.

Our People

Sizani is run by teachers, mentors and volunteer digital literacy facilitators who show up every week for our learners.
